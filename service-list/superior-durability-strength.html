When it comes to industrial shipping and heavy logistics, standard packaging often falls short. Paperystick Industries provides Superior Durability & Strength packaging solutions engineered to withstand the toughest supply chain challenges. Our heavy-duty corrugated boxes are constructed using high-grade kraft paper and multi-wall combinations (5-ply, 7-ply, and 9-ply) to offer exceptional stacking strength and burst resistance.

We understand that product damage during transit not only incurs costs but also damages your brand reputation. That's why our robust packaging is designed to absorb impact, resist compression, and protect against environmental factors like moisture and humidity. Whether you are shipping automotive parts, heavy machinery, or fragile electronics, we have the structural expertise to keep your goods secure.

Built for Heavy Duty

Our durable packaging solutions offer critical advantages for high-value and heavy goods:

High Load Capacity

Engineered to support significant weight without buckling or collapsing under pressure.

Impact Resistance

Superior cushioning properties protect contents from drops, shocks, and vibrations.

Moisture Barrier

Optional coatings and treatments prevent moisture damage during sea or humid transport.

Cost Efficient

Lightweight alternative to wood or metal crates without compromising on protection.

Engineering & Quality Assurance

We utilize rigorous testing standards to ensure every box meets your durability requirements:

  • Edge Crush Test (ECT) for stacking strength
  • Bursting Strength Test (BST) for containment
  • Drop Testing for impact simulation
  • Vibration Testing for transit reliability
  • Custom Internal Partitions & Cushioning
