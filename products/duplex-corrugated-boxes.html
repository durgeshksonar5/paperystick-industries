Paperystick Industries offers premium Duplex Corrugated Boxes, combining the strength of corrugated cardboard with the high-quality finish of duplex board. These boxes are the ultimate solution for brands looking to make a visual impact on the shelf. By laminating a printed duplex sheet onto a corrugated flute, we create packaging that is both robust enough for shipping and attractive enough for retail display.

Our duplex corrugated boxes are widely used in consumer electronics, household appliances, toys, and food products where branding is crucial. The smooth outer surface allows for superior offset printing, resulting in vibrant colors, sharp text, and photographic-quality images. Inside, the corrugated structural layer provides the necessary cushioning and stacking strength to protect your products.
