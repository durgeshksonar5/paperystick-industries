When standard corrugated boxes aren't enough, Paperystick Industries delivers extreme strength with our Heavy Duty Containers. Engineered for bulk cargo, heavy industrial parts, and export shipments, these containers are designed to withstand the rigorous demands of the global supply chain. Often replacing wooden crates and metal bins, our heavy-duty solutions offer a lightweight yet incredibly robust alternative that significantly lowers freight costs.

Constructed from double or triple-wall corrugated board (AAA, AAC flutes), these pallet boxes offer exceptional stacking strength and puncture resistance. They are ideal for transporting granular products, chemical resins, automotive components, and fresh produce. Fully customizable with drop-front panels for easy access or integrated pallet feet, our containers ensure your heavy loads arrive intact and secure.

Key Features

Built for performance in the toughest environments:

Extreme Load Bearing

Capable of holding over 1000kg of loose or bulk product without bulging or collapsing.

Stacking Strength

Reinforced corners and high-grade fluting allow for safe multi-level stacking in warehouses.

Export Ready

ISPM-15 exempt, making international customs clearance faster and smoother than wood packaging.

Space Saving

Delivered flat to minimize storage space before use, unlike rigid drums or crates.

Applications & Uses

Our heavy-duty containers are the preferred choice for:

  • Bulk Chemicals: Octabins for granules, powders, and resins.
  • Automotive: CKD kits and heavy engine components.
  • Agriculture: Large produce bins for watermelons, pumpkins, and potatoes.
  • Textiles: Large volume cap boxes for yarn spools and fabric rolls.
