Paperystick Industries specializes in high-quality Locking Boxes (often known as mailer boxes with locking tabs), designed to provide superior security and a premium unboxing experience. These boxes feature a smart self-locking mechanism that secures the lid without the need for adhesive tape, making them an eco-friendly and stylish choice for e-commerce, retail, and subscription boxes.

Engineered for durability and presentation, our locking boxes are made from sturdy corrugated board that protects contents during transit while offering a clean, professional canvas for your brand. The front flaps lock securely into the side walls, ensuring the box stays closed until it reaches the customer. Whether shipping electronics, cosmetics, or apparel, our locking boxes combine structural integrity with aesthetic appeal.

Key Features

Why our Locking Boxes are the preferred choice for modern shipping:

Secure Closure

Self-locking tabs keep the box firmly closed without the need for extra tape.

Double Wall Protection

The front and side walls are reinforced by double/triple folding for extra strength.

Presentation Ready

Opens like a gift box, enhancing the customer's unboxing experience.

Easy Assembly

Ships flat to save space and can be assembled in seconds without tools.
