High-quality corrugated sheets are the foundation of robust packaging. At Paperystick Industries, we manufacture premium corrugated sheets available in various sizes, grades, and flute profiles (A, B, C, E, F) to suit your specific converting needs. Whether you are a box manufacturer requiring raw sheets or a business needing pads and separators for palletizing, our sheets deliver unmatched strength-to-weight ratios and versatility.

Manufactured using high-grade kraft paper and state-of-the-art corrugators, our sheets ensure excellent bonding and structural integrity. We offer 3-ply (Single Wall), 5-ply (Double Wall), and 7-ply (Triple Wall) options, customizable to your required Burst Strength (BS) and Grammage (GSM). Our strict quality control ensures uniform caliper and moisture content, making our sheets ideal for high-speed automatic converting lines.
