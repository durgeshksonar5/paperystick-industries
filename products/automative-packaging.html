The automotive industry demands packaging that is as robust and reliable as the vehicles themselves. At Paperystick Industries, we specialize in high-performance automotive packaging solutions designed to protect heavy, high-value, and sensitive components through complex supply chains. From CKD (Completely Knocked Down) and SKD (Semi Knocked Down) kits to individual spare parts, our corrugated solutions offer superior protection and cost efficiency.

We understand the critical nature of "Just-in-Time" delivery lines where damaged parts can halt production. Our heavy-duty corrugated boxes, reinforced with custom engineered inserts, replace traditional wooden crates, offering a lighter, sustainable, and export-compliant alternative. Whether you are shipping engine blocks, windshields, or sensitive electronics, we engineer the perfect fit.

Key Features for Auto Parts

Our automotive packaging is engineered for the specific challenges of the sector:

Heavy Load Bearing

Multi-wall corrugated board (AAA or AAC flutes) capable of supporting loads up to 1000kg.

Corrosion Protection

Integrated VCI (Volatile Corrosion Inhibitor) liners to prevent rust on metallic parts during sea transit.

Surface Protection

Soft non-abrasive coatings and foam inserts to protect painted and finished surfaces from scratches.

Space Optimization

Collapsible designs reduce return freight costs and optimize warehouse storage space.

Applications & Capabilities

We provide specialized packaging for:

  • Powertrain: Heavy-duty solutions for engines, transmissions, and axles.
  • Exterior/Interior: Large format boxes for bumpers, dashboards, and door panels.
  • Lighting & Glass: Shock-absorbing inserts for headlamps, mirrors, and windshields.
  • Aftermarket: Branded retail-ready boxes for brake pads, filters, and accessories.
