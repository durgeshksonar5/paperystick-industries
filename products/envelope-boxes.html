Paperystick Industries offers high-quality corrugated Envelope Boxes, the perfect hybrid between a standard envelope and a rigid box. Designed for e-commerce and courier shipments, these boxes provide superior corner protection and structural rigidity compared to paper or poly mailers. They are ideal for shipping flat items that require extra care, such as books, documents, photographs, and artwork, ensuring they arrive at their destination without bends or creases.

Our envelope boxes are manufactured from tough micro-flute corrugated cardboard (E-flute or F-flute), which makes them incredibly lightweight yet strong. Featuring a height-adjustable design, they can expand slightly to accommodate thicker products while remaining compact enough to qualify for lower postal rates. With options for self-sealing adhesive strips and tear-open tabs, they offer a frustration-free unboxing experience for your customers.
