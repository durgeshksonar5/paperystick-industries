Corrugated trays are the unsung heroes of supply chain efficiency, providing essential support for transporting cans, bottles, fruits, and vegetables. At Paperystick Industries, we specialize in high-strength corrugated trays designed for open-top visibility and easy handling. Whether you need stackable fruit trays for agriculture or wrap-around trays for beverage canning lines, our solutions ensure your products remain stable and secure from production line to retail shelf.

Our trays are engineered for performance. We use high-quality kraft liners to ensure moisture resistance—crucial for agricultural produce—and superior compression strength for stacking capabilities. Designed for both manual and machine-erected packing lines, our trays can be customized with ventilation holes, hand holes, and high-impact graphics to serve as effective shelf-ready packaging.

Why Choose Our Corrugated Trays?

From cost-effective transit solutions to brand-enhancing retail displays, our trays offer multiple advantages:

Stacking Strength

Reinforced corners and high-grade fluting allow for secure stacking of heavy loads.

Product Visibility

Open-top design ensures products are easily identified and accessible on shelves.

Moisture Resistance

Specialized coatings and kraft papers available for cold chain and agricultural use.

Cost Effective

Uses less material than full enclosed boxes while providing necessary stability.

Applications & Features

We cater to diverse industry needs:

  • Beverage Trays: Shrink-wrap ready trays for cans and bottles.
  • Agricultural Trays: Ventilated designs for fresh produce airflow.
  • Shelf-Ready: Perforated designs that convert from transit to display.
  • Machine Erect: Precision cut for high-speed automatic tray formers.
  • Self-Locking: Glue-free manual assembly options available.
