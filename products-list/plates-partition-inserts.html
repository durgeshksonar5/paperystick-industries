At Paperystick Industries, we know that product safety often relies on what is inside the box. Our range of corrugated plates, partitions, and inserts provides the critical internal structure needed to separate, cushion, and protect your goods during transit. Whether you are shipping fragile glass bottles, precision engineered automotive parts, or delicate electronics, our custom separators limit movement and prevent impact damage.

These internal packaging components are key to maximizing box strength and stacking efficiency. By adding vertical support within the carton, our partitions can significantly increase compression strength, allowing you to stack pallets higher with confidence. We offer fully customizable grid sizes, layer pads, and die-cut inserts tailored to the exact geometry of your product.

Key Components

Enhance your packaging performance with our specialized inserts:

Partitions (Grid)

Interlocking cardboard strips that create individual cells for bottles, jars, and cylindrical items.

Layer Pads (Plates)

Flat sheets placed between layers of products to distribute weight and increase stacking stability.

Die-Cut Inserts

Custom-shaped holders that lock products in place, providing superior presentation and shock absorption.

Z-Dividers

Simple zigzag folded sheets used to separate long items or provide side cushioning.

Benefits & Applications

Our structural components serve vital roles across industries:

  • Glass & Beverages: Prevent bottle-to-bottle contact and breakage.
  • Automotive: Separate multiple heavy parts in a single bin (dunnage).
  • Electronics: Secure placement for devices and accessories in retail boxes.
  • Cost Efficient: Often eliminates the need for expensive bubble wrap or foam.
